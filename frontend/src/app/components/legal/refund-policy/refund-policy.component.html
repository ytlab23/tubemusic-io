Refund Policy

We want TubeMusic.io PRO to work great for you. If it doesn’t, you get your money back — simple as that. No hoops, no fine-print loopholes.

1. Our promise

If TubeMusic.io PRO is not delivering value for you for any reason, we’ll refund your last billed month, even if you ask on day 29 of the cycle. You don’t need to justify the request. Reply to a billing email or contact us — we’ll process the refund within a few business days.

2. Cancel anytime

Subscriptions are month-to-month. You can cancel at any time from your account area. After cancellation, your PRO benefits remain active until the end of the current billing period — there are no long-term commitments and no cancellation fees.

3. How to request a refund

Reach out via the channels listed on our Contact page with the email address tied to your subscription. Refunds are returned to the original payment method through our subscription provider; the timing depends on your bank or card issuer.

4. Edge cases

Refunds cover the most recent billing month only. We don’t backdate refunds for periods you used PRO without contacting us. Repeated refund requests on the same account may be declined to prevent abuse, but our default stance is to err on the side of customer trust.

5. Free features stay free

The core music copyright checker, our copyright-free music gallery, and a limited daily MP3 download quota are free for everyone — no card required. PRO simply unlocks unlimited downloads, AI music generation, full library access, and human support. If PRO isn’t for you, the free experience continues to be 100% free.

6. Questions?

We read every message. If you have questions about billing, cancellation, or refunds, the fastest way to reach us is the Contact page or any of the social channels in the footer.
